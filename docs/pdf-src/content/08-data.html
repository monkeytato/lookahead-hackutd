What could not be obtained

  • IEEE 493 Annex Q tables and IEEE 3006.8 (paywalled): the Gold Book generator figure is used only through WP75's citation.
  • Manufacturer chiller capacity-versus-ambient tables (they live in selection software), so the derate per °C is an assumption, U(1.0, 2.0) %/°C A.
  • Any measured data-center leak-spread data; any base rate of false temperature or leak alarms; full texts of Zhang and VanGilder 2011 and Lin et al. 2014.
  • No local PDF renderer was available, so PDF numbers were read through a web-fetch tool. WP179 Table 2 was reconstructed from scrambled PDF text and must be checked against the PDF before it is quoted; this document quotes no value from that table.
